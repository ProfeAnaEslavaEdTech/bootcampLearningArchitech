Origin

Born from a masterclass.

Not taken from a slide — rebuilt into something reusable.

Formative attribution +

UX Writing principles derived from the masterclass by Antonio Estévez Funes, Conversational Designer at BBVA — Instructional Design School, UPV, 6 Nov 2026. The session covered the difference between copywriting and UX Writing; the three principles (clear, consistent, useful); voice versus tone; plain language; CTA patterns; error feedback; and the relationship between UX Writing and WCAG accessibility.

Synthesis, Travel Good case study, and all extensions: Ana Eslava-Graterol's own adaptation. Not reviewed or endorsed by Antonio Estévez Funes, BBVA, or the UPV.

The A/B test data point (33% → 59% login comprehension) was shared publicly during the session. If cited outside this portfolio, attribute it to the session, not to CUXedTech.
